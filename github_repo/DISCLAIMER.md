## What This Tool Is For

NetWARRIOR is a professional security testing platform for:

- **Authorized penetration testing** — testing your own infrastructure or client systems
  under a signed scope-of-work agreement
- **Red team exercises** — simulating adversary behavior in controlled environments
- **Security research** — understanding attack techniques to build better defenses
- **CTF (Capture the Flag) competitions** — practicing on intentionally vulnerable systems
- **Network stress testing** — load testing infrastructure you own and operate
- **Security education** — learning how attacks work to understand how to defend against them
